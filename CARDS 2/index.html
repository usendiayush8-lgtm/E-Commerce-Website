lion

The lion (Lion) is known as the "King of the Jungle," although it mainly lives in grasslands and savannas rather than jungles.

Tiger

The tiger (Tiger) is the largest wild cat in the world. It is famous for its orange coat with black stripes. The tiger is the national animal of India.

Elephant

The Elephant (Elephant) is the largest land mammal. It is known for its long trunk, large ears, and tusks.

Giraffe

The Giraffe (Giraffe) is the tallest land animal. Its long neck helps it eat leaves from tall trees.

Zebra

The Zebra (Zebra) is famous for its black-and-white striped coat. Every zebra has a unique stripe pattern.
